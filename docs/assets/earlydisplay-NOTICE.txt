NeoSync startup graphics

This library is based on FancyModLoader earlydisplay 4.0.44 by Forge Development
LLC, NeoForged, and contributors, licensed under LGPL-2.1-only. The Java classes,
service registration, module identity, and upstream ownership notices are unchanged.
NeoSync contributors replaced neoforged_icon.png, fox_running.png, and squirrel.png
with NeoSync's original N mark. The historical resource names are required by FML.

Matching source archives are distributed as NeoSync-<version>-neoforge-<base>-earlydisplay-sources.jar.
The graphics and packaging recipe are available in the matching NeoSync source tag:
https://github.com/enderliker/NeoSync
